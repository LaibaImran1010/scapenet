Deep learning semester project

Recognise natural scenes with two deep learning models

Upload a photo and ScapeNet tells you if it shows buildings, forest, glacier, mountain, sea or street. Test our self-trained CNN, a pretrained MobileNetV2, or both side by side.

  • Public Kaggle dataset
  • Open-source code and weights
  • Tested on 3,000 unseen photos

How it works

The model runs inside your browser, so your photo never leaves your device.

Dataset

Secondary dataset from Kaggle, downloaded through the Kaggle API.

NameIntel Image Classification (Kaggle)
Classesbuildings, forest, glacier, mountain, sea, street
Images150 x 150 pixel colour photos
TrainingAbout 14,000 photos, split 85% training and 15% validation
Testing3,000 photos never used for training (437 buildings, 474 forest, 553 glacier, 525 mountain, 510 sea, 501 street)

Two models, one dataset

The pretrained model was our reference. Our final model is the CNN we trained ourselves.

Self-trained CNN (final)Pretrained MobileNetV2 (reference)
Starting weightsRandom, no pretrainingImageNet pretrained
Architecture4 blocks of 32, 64, 128, 256 filters (two 3x3 convolutions, batch normalisation, ReLU, max pooling), then pooling, dropout, dense 128, dropout, softmaxMobileNetV2 base, global pooling, dropout 0.3, softmax with 6 outputs
Training38 epochs in two runs (Adam 1e-3, then 3e-4), early stopping16 epochs in two stages (Adam 1e-3, then 1e-5 fine-tuning)
AugmentationFlip, rotation, zoomFlip, rotation, zoom
Test accuracy84.1%90.4%
Test loss0.46440.2533
Weights file14.6 MB22 MB
